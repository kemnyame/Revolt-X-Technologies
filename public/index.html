A few useful answers

A few things
you might want to know.

How to start, what to expect and what happens after launch.

Do I need to understand technology to get started?

No. Tell us what is difficult, what takes too much time or what you want to build. We will help you work out a practical way forward.

Can you work with software we already use?

Yes. We can review what you have, suggest improvements and connect systems where it makes sense. The plan depends on the tools and access available.

Will it work on phones and tablets?

We design for the devices your users need. Responsive layouts, readable text and easy-to-use controls are part of the planning and testing.

How much does a project cost?

The price depends on the work involved, the number of users, connections to other systems and support needs. We agree the scope and quote before work begins.

How long will it take?

That depends on the work involved, the content or data available and any connections to other tools. After the first discussion, we agree a plan with milestones and explain what we need from you.

What happens after launch?

We can help with training, maintenance and improvements. We agree the support you need as part of the project plan.
